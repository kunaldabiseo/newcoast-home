01

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NewCoast Commercial Pty Ltd trading as NewCoast Business Brokers (“NewCoast”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information.

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, where they apply to us.

02

Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you deal with us. It may include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • What you are interested in, such as selling, buying or an appraisal, and any message you send us
  • Details you give us when you sign a confidentiality agreement
  • Business information supplied by vendors for an appraisal or listing, such as trading, lease, staff and supplier details
03

How we collect it

We collect most information directly from you: when you call or email us, complete a form on our website, sign a confidentiality agreement or meet with our brokers. Sometimes we receive information from a vendor, buyer or their advisers as part of a sale.

04

How we use it

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and give you the information you asked for
  • Appraise, list, market and sell businesses and commercial property
  • Provide buyers with listings and information memoranda after a confidentiality agreement is signed
  • Add registered buyers to our buyer list for similar listings (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Meet our legal and regulatory obligations
05

Who we share it with

We only share personal information where it is needed for the purpose it was collected, for example:

  • Vendors and prospective buyers involved in a sale, subject to confidentiality obligations
  • Legal, accounting and finance advisers acting for the parties to a sale
  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as email and IT providers
  • Government bodies or others where the law requires or permits it

We do not sell personal information.

06

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and to destroy or de-identify it when it is no longer needed.

07

Marketing and unsubscribing

If you are on our registered buyer list, we may send you listings of businesses similar to the ones you have shown interest in. You can ask to be removed at any time by replying to the email or contacting us.

08

Access and correction

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

09

Our website

Our website may use cookies and third-party services, such as web fonts and maps, to work properly. These services may receive technical information, such as your IP address and browser type. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

10

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page.

12
