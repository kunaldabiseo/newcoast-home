Buyer guide

What to Look For When Buying a
Hospitality Business

Five things we help every buyer understand before they commit.

Buyer checklist01 / 05

  1. Check 01 of 05

    The Listing Price

    Our appraisal process is more thorough than other business brokerages. We don’t just list a business at any price, we make sure that price reflects the true business operation. Our buyers trust the due diligence we do upfront when appraising a hospitality business, which includes a thorough assessment of the lease terms, trading and financial history.

  2. Check 02 of 05

    The Lease and Premises

    Part of the NewCoast appraisal process involves an assessment of the current and future lease terms, to ensure any limitations are addressed and factored into the listing price. We work with our vendors to secure strong lease terms, address any council or strata limitations and ascertain future rent and outgoing costs, to make the lease assignment process easier for the new owner pre-settlement. As a buyer, you will have a complete and transparent understanding of the lease.

  3. Check 03 of 05

    The Due Diligence

    Although NewCoast completes a thorough market appraisal before listing the business, we encourage our buyers to complete their own due diligence before exchanging contracts. We work with the vendor to supply you with all information relevant to the business operation and address any questions you may have. We want you to feel as confident as possible, so you know exactly how to plan and what to expect when taking over the business yourself.

  4. Check 04 of 05

    Your Hospitality Experience

    NewCoast Business Brokers will match your hospitality experience with the right business. Whether its a fully managed, owner operated or franchise opportunity, we will help guide you on which business model best suits your experience. Buying an existing hospitality business also involves an application to the landlord as per the Retail Leases Act in NSW. We will help you navigate this process and ensure you have all relevant documentation ready to demonstrate your hospitality background.

  5. Check 05 of 05

    Handover and Settlement Terms

    We help our buyers negotiate the terms of settlement with the vendor, and guide all parties from contract exchange to settlement. Some of the key hospitality considerations for a buyer are any agreed pre and post settlement training terms, employee entitlements, liquor license transfers, unredeemed gift vouchers or function deposits held by the business, equipment inspections, stock valuation and supplier agreements. During this time, we encourage all discussions to remain confidential so that customers are only notified at an agreed date, and staff are notified when the sale is securely committed.
