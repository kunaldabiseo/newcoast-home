Our story

The NewCoast Story

The Beginning

NewCoast Business Brokers was founded by Anthony Manks after recognising that hospitality and retail business sales require niche and specific expertise. Focusing solely on the retail sector, including hospitality businesses, means that our clients can feel comfortable knowing their broker has a specialised set of skills to find them the right buyer at the right price.

Our Location

Originally, NewCoast was intended as a Newcastle, Central Coast focused brokerage, where Anthony has spent his professional career. However, the demand for a broker with a personal approach, was missing in Sydney. As a result, Anthony decided it was time to offer business brokerage services in Sydney and bring back that ‘local’ feeling to our city friends as well.

Our Niche

NewCoast offers all the services of a regular business brokerage and commercial real estate agency, however our niche industry focus means our database and network is strongly aligned to our clients’ goals. This helps us achieve quicker, more desirable results.

Our Promise

There are many reasons why a business owner may choose to sell, however, we know a hospitality business requires a lot of passion and skill. We want to honour the years of hard work that you’ve dedicated and create an exit strategy that helps you move into the next chapter seamlessly. For our buyers, we want to set you up for success from the beginning. Whether it’s your first cafe or your third restaurant, Anthony Manks wanted the transition into your new venture to be exciting and easy.

Today

With over 25 years hospitality and sales experience, Anthony Manks and his team are ready to assist you with a strategic, intentional, listing plan for your hospitality or retail business.

What guides us

Our Values

Professionalism

We are committed to upholding the highest standards of professionalism through every aspect of a sale. We always comply with industry regulation as well as protecting client information with care, maintaining privacy and confidentiality as a priority. We act responsibly and respectfully, in the best interests of both vendors and buyers.

Integrity

At NewCoast Business Brokers we pride ourselves on providing full disclosure and transparency with every sale. Our clients trust our processes and appreciate our honest and reliable approach. Any party that interacts with our brokers can be assured that integrity shapes how we negotiate and represent a business.

Communication

All clients receive regular, timely updates, straightforward explanations and guidance when dealing with our business brokers. We listen to our clients and our communication approach keeps all parties informed, feeling supported and confident as they progress through each stage of the sale transaction.

Read Anthony’s Story

Anthony Manks, NewCoast’s principal and licensee in charge, owned and operated cafes and restaurants in NSW for over 20 years. Coming from a sales background and having a passion for food and customer service, Anthony naturally found himself in the hospitality and retail industry. Anthony decided to become a licensed business broker after the sale of his own businesses, where he saw an opportunity to create an improved and niche experience for business owners in hospitality. He felt that selling hospitality businesses required a true understanding of the wins and challenges that only a hospitality business owner would understand.

Recently, Anthony broke his own record and sold over 50 businesses in 40 months. As an award-winning broker, Anthony has a 100% client satisfaction rate with a reputation for achieving businesses under offer within 30 days. Anthony decided to create NewCoast Business Brokers so he can assist more businesses while still maintaining the same local, personal connection he has with all his clients.

Read Kate’s Story

That last name looks familiar! Yes, Kate and Anthony are related, but this only makes their process stronger as they work closely together with every client. However, Kate comes with her own set of highly valuable skills in hospitality, business marketing and law. Kate grew up in a world of hospitality and is not only a licensed business broker, but an admitted Australian Legal Practitioner in NSW after completing a Bachelor of Laws in Sydney.

Before she became a licensed business broker and real estate agent, Kate worked closely with business owners on their marketing strategy and branding. Kate directed and operated a branding agency for many years, helping business owners increase the value of their new and existing businesses, and helping them to navigate a quickly evolving world of technology and social media. Now, Kate’s extensive knowledge and experience in sales, marketing and law, is a highly valuable tool in achieving the best possible results for her clients.
