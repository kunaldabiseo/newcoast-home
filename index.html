Frequently Asked Questions

Still unsure about something? Ask us directly. The first conversation is free and confidential.

Ask a question
How do I know what my hospitality business is worth? +

The value of a business depends on several factors, including financial performance, location, lease terms, assets, customer base and future potential. Our appraisal combines a key metric assessment, a confidential site inspection and a Zoom consultation to give you an evidence-based view of your current market value.

Will the sale of my business remain confidential? +

Yes. Enquiries are qualified, information is released only after a mandatory non-disclosure agreement is signed, and inspections are arranged outside business hours with your approval. Customers are told at an agreed date, and staff once the sale is securely committed.

How long does it take to sell a hospitality business? +

Every sale moves at its own pace, depending on the business, the lease and landlord approval, and buyer finance. We’ll talk through the stages and what affects timing for your business in your first conversation.

What happens with the lease when a business is sold? +

Buying an existing hospitality business involves an application to the landlord under the Retail Leases Act in NSW. We assess current and future lease terms during the appraisal, address council or strata limitations, and help buyers prepare the documents that show their hospitality background.

Can you sell my commercial property as well as the business? +

Yes. As licensed real estate agents, we can appraise, list and sell your commercial property in combination with your business, or sell the property separately using our database of verified buyers.

Do you only work with hospitality businesses? +

Hospitality is our specialty, from cafés and restaurants to bars, takeaways and franchises. We also work with retail businesses such as clothing stores, health and fitness, playcentres and beauty businesses.
