Confidentiality Agreement

Sign a confidentiality agreement so that you can receive our business listings and trading information for a particular business you’re interested in.

01

What Is a Confidentiality Agreement?

A confidentiality agreement is the same as a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) and these phrases are often used interchangeably. It is often required in the business brokering industry before sensitive information can be provided to a prospective buyer. This type of agreement ensures that the sale is kept confidential, intellectual property is safeguarded and ensures that the appropriate communication methods are used between the broker, vendor and prospective buyer.

02

Why Do We Use a Confidentiality Agreement?

Our process is designed to be discrete and confidential to protect the identity of our clients and their businesses. This is because we handle sensitive personal and financial information related to each business that we list and it’s important that this information is handled with care.

As a business owners themselves, most of our interested buyers know what it’s like to manage staff and customer confidence, as well as ensuring their intellectual property and trading information is not put at risk during this sales process. Therefore, we believe it’s important for any interested party to sign a confidentiality agreement to ensure any disclosed information remains confidential throughout the sales process.

However, we understand and want to ensure that our buyers have access to this information in order to make an informed decision and complete their due diligence effectively. Therefore, this confidentiality agreement allows both processes to occur simultaneously.

03

What To Expect as an Interested Buyer?

We can discuss a specific business listing or if you would like some general recommendations on available businesses that might suit your experience, budget and goals. Once you sign the confidentiality agreement we can provide you with an information memorandum on the particular business you’d like to view.

You will also be added to our registered buyer list which means you will receive listings of similar businesses when they become available. You will be able to unsubscribe from this list at any time. We think this is an important addition, since you can compare business listings in your desired location and will be fully aware of all options available to you at the time. We will help guide you on what type of business operation we think matches your preferences and position.

We list businesses across Newcastle, Central Coast, Sydney, Mid-North Coast and Regional NSW. We can help you find an owner-operated business model, franchise or fully managed operation depending on what you’re looking for. We encourage you to sign our confidentiality agreement so that you will have complete access to our listings as they become available and can start discussing your plans with our team in more detail.
