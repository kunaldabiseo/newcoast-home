Is Now The Perfect Time To Sell?

This is usually the first question our clients ask. People decide to sell their business for many reasons and the right time is always when you and your business are ready. To determine when your business is ready, NewCoast Business Brokers always begin the process by understanding our client’s market position and conducting a thorough market appraisal.

The market appraisal will help identify any opportunities for improvement along with the strengths of the business, helping to prepare you for when you’re ready to sell.

Good businesses are always in demand. NewCoast Business Brokers has a strong database of qualified buyers always ready to purchase. Reach out to our team to have a confidential conversation today.

Maximise Your Sale Price With Hospitality Experts

Hospitality is a dynamic industry and being aware of all the factors that contribute to a successful business requires expertise. Working with NewCoast Business Brokers is a reliable way to maximise your sale price, because we understand what buyers value most in a hospitality business. We become familiar with your key metrics, such as cost of goods margins, seasonal staff requirements, wages and payroll, current and future lease terms, and more.

Choosing a business broker specialised in hospitality, means you’re choosing an expert that can defend your business value and have informed negotiations with prospective buyers on your behalf.

Support From Listing to Settlement

Our team of highly experienced hospitality brokers will guide you through every step of the sales process until settlement. This begins from our appraisal process to then listing the business with a strategic marketing plan, qualifying buyers, negotiating, conducting confidential inspections, navigating landlord approval and lease assignment, arranging handover and finalising settlement terms.

We aim to create a smooth experience from beginning to end, so that you can focus on your exit strategy and your next journey. For more information on what to expect, see the sales process below.

Our process

Hospitality Business Sale Process

A simple, transparent 7-step process designed to make selling your hospitality business straightforward.

  2. 01

    Get In Touch

    NewCoast Business Brokers is ready to assist you. Get in touch for a free confidential consultation about your hospitality business, or send an email to contact@newcoastbrokers.com.au.

  3. 02

    Complete a Business Appraisal

    A key metric assessment, a confidential site inspection, a Zoom consultation and an appraisal overview written for your business, giving you an evidence-based view of its current market value.

  4. 03

    List Your Business

    Marketing tailored to capture a specific buyer profile. Every enquiry is qualified, and information is released only once a non-disclosure agreement has been signed.

  5. 04

    Receive Offers On Your Business

    Offers come from buyers whose financial position, experience and goals have been checked against your business. The appraisal lets us defend its value through negotiation.

  6. 05

    Lease and Landlord Application

    Buying an existing hospitality business involves an application to the landlord under the Retail Leases Act in NSW. We help prepare it and work through any council or strata limitations.

  7. 06

    Facilitating Business Handover

    Agreed training terms, employee entitlements, liquor licence transfers, gift vouchers and function deposits, equipment inspections, stock valuation and supplier agreements.

  8. 07

    Settlement

    We guide all parties from contract exchange to settlement. Customers are told at an agreed date, and staff once the sale is securely committed.

  9. Ready to start with step one?
